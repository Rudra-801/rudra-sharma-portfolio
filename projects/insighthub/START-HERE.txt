The login/signup flow now runs through the Node/Express backend. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed, sessions use signed JWTs, /api/auth/me validates refresh/reload sessions, and registered users persist in backend/data/users.json.

MongoDB is optional for the authentication demo; configure MONGODB_URI when you want the included article API to use MongoDB.
